PainTeX

Paint, mark up and drop typeset equations onto any screenshot, right on your Mac.

Get it on the Mac App Store macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Type the math. See it typeset where you clicked.

Pick the equation tool, click anywhere on your image and type LaTeX. A live preview appears on the canvas as you type. Paste straight from a .tex file: $…$, \[…\], equation, align, cases, matrices and arrays all work.

Everything you reach for when marking up results

Made for engineers, students and anyone who screenshots a plot and needs to say “look here.” It works like the classic Paint you already know.

1

Numbered markers

Click to drop 1, 2, 3… each perfectly centered in a circle. Set the next number or let Undo rewind it.

2

Arrows and shapes

Arrow, double arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, rounded rectangle and triangle. Color 1 outlines, Color 2 fills.

3

Text on the image

Click and type right where you want it. Change font, size and color while you type.

4

Move anything later

Text, equations, markers and shapes stay movable. Drag, nudge with the arrow keys, delete, or double-click to edit.

5

Paint and edit

Brush, pencil, eraser, fill bucket, crop, resize, rotate and a coarse or fine grid. Each tool remembers its own size.

6

Paste in, copy out

Paste a screenshot straight from the clipboard, then copy the result into your email, report or slides.

Private by design

PainTeX runs entirely on your Mac. It never connects to the internet, has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
